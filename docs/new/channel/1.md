*UN tests sovereign AI cloud — without Big Tech*

> Axios · Aug 25, 2026

The *UNDP* and *DFINITY Foundation* are launching a pilot to help governments build AI-powered apps *without relying solely on U.S.-based cloud providers*.

*Why it matters*
Governments are questioning how much control they keep over sensitive citizen data when it sits on infrastructure run by foreign tech giants.

*Driving the news*
• UNDP will select *5 countries* for initial pilots within ~1 month
• Each project targets a specific government problem
• Builds on DFINITY's work with *Pakistan* — several apps already live on national digital infrastructure

*The big picture*
As countries rush to adopt AI, some want public services that do not depend on AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure for underlying data and compute.

One use case: analyze *200,000 medical scans* with AI while keeping health data under government control.

*Between the lines*
"If someone could flick a kill switch and knock out your cloud and your AI, your country is going to grind to a halt."
— *Dominic Williams*, DFINITY founder

*What's next*
UNDP will evaluate pilots on cost, ease of use, and whether officials want to scale — learning where decentralized AI and cloud actually make sense.

*Why this matters for ICP*
Sovereign infrastructure is not a slogan. It is where apps, data, and compute stay under rules you control — not a hyperscaler in another jurisdiction.
