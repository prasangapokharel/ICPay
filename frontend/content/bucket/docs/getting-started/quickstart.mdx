## Upload your first file

1. [Create a bucket](/bucket) in ICPay and copy your **bucket ID** and **API key**.
2. Install the SDK for your language (npm or pip above).
3. Follow the [TypeScript SDK](/bucket/docs/sdk/typescript) or [Python SDK](/bucket/docs/sdk/python) guide to upload.

<Callout title="Tip">
Public buckets expose CDN URLs immediately after upload. Private buckets require a signed download call.
</Callout>
