## Storage

- Images, documents, code, archives, audio, and fonts (see Pricing for the full list).
- Native format up to 10 MB. Large files are chunked automatically (IC ingress limit is 2 MiB per call).
- Max **10 MB** per upload.

## Security & billing

- Encrypted at rest with per-bucket keys.
- 30-day plans — renew anytime, time stacks.
